## Send feedback from the Help menu

**Help → Send Feedback…** opens a short form in your browser, already filled in
with SiRPS and its version, and sends a problem or an idea straight to the
people who build SiRPS.

The first time, the form asks for your email address and sends a six-digit
code to confirm it; after that, that browser sends straight through. You can
add a screenshot by choosing a file or pasting one into the page.
