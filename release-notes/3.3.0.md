## Your license, in the app

**License…** (in the SiRPS menu on a Mac, under Help on Windows) shows this
computer's Decibel One license. Add the `.d1license` file from your Decibel One
email with **Add License File…**, or drop it on the window; on a Mac you can
also double-click it in Finder. One file covers every Decibel One app you've
licensed, and every one of them on the computer reads it. **Buy a License…**
opens decibelone.com, and **Remove License…** takes it off this computer.

SiRPS is free, with every feature, for students, teachers and non-pro users.
When you're paid for the work you use it on, it needs a license. Nothing is
ever locked. About says whose license it is, or "Student, teacher and non-pro
use".

## Send feedback from the Help menu

**Help → Send Feedback…** opens a short form in your browser, already filled in
with SiRPS and its version, and sends a problem or an idea straight to the
people who build SiRPS.

The first time, the form asks for your email address and sends a six-digit
code to confirm it; after that, that browser sends straight through. You can
add a screenshot by choosing a file or pasting one into the page.
