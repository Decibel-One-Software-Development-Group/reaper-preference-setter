What it does

  • Preferences — startup behaviour, project template, default save and media paths, where peak files live. Set once, the same on every machine.
  • DiGiCo → Reaper CSV — drop a .ses from the console and get a track-name CSV for the J&T Live Recording Template's PATCH IMPORT. Channel names, stereo pairs and patch order come straight out of the session.

What you need

  • For the CSV: a DiGiCo Quantum (SDQ) console on software v22 or later, with your Copy Audio routing saved as a preset named Extract for Reaper
  • macOS 11 Big Sur or later (Apple Silicon or Intel), or Windows 10 or later
  • REAPER closed while preferences are applied — the app checks and tells you
